## Alaska Airlines Flight 1282

Three dates mark the incident, the FAA response and the NTSB finding.

- **5 January 2024.** A door plug separated in flight and the aircraft returned to Portland. [NTSB investigation](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/Pages/DCA24MA063.aspx)
- **6 January 2024.** The FAA temporarily grounded certain Boeing 737-9 MAX aircraft. [FAA grounding updates](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/updates-grounding-boeing-737-9-max-aircraft)
- **24 June 2025.** The NTSB announced its probable-cause finding. [NTSB findings](https://www.ntsb.gov/news/press-releases/Pages/NR20250624.aspx)

The grounding order describes the immediate safety response. The probable-cause finding is the later result of the investigation. [Full timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/incidents/alaska-airlines-1282-door-plug/)

## Three patents for early record players

Edison's phonograph and Berliner's gramophone left separate records of how sound was captured and played back.

- **19 Feb 1878. Edison's phonograph.** Thomas Edison received U.S. patent 200,521 for a phonograph that recorded sound as indentations in a moving surface. [U.S. patent 200,521](https://patents.google.com/patent/US200521A/en)
- **8 Nov 1887. Berliner's gramophone.** Emile Berliner received U.S. patent 372,786 for a gramophone. The record describes tracking a groove to reproduce sound. [U.S. patent 372,786](https://patents.google.com/patent/US372786A/en)
- **15 May 1888. Making a sound record.** A later Berliner patent described making a sound record by etching a trace into a prepared metal surface. [U.S. patent 382,790](https://patents.google.com/patent/US382790A/en)

The dates identify patent grants. Look to sales records for when the products reached buyers. [Full timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/companies/early-record-player-history/)

## How the Beethoven films changed settings

Three entries show how the films kept the St. Bernard at the centre while changing the people and situations around him.

- **3 Apr 1992. The Newton family.** The original film follows George and Alice Newton's household after a St. Bernard puppy arrives. [American Film Institute catalogue](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/59190-BEETHOVEN)
- **2000. Another Newton household.** Beethoven's 3rd follows Richard Newton and his family on a road trip with the dog. [Universal Pictures home release](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/beethovens-3rd)
- **2008. A film set.** Beethoven's Big Break puts the dog in a story about an animal handler and a Hollywood production. [Universal Pictures home release](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/beethovens-big-break)

The selection tracks changes in setting across three films. The full timeline includes the intervening entries. [Full timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/companies/beethoven-film-series-history/)

## Elementary's CBS run

The pilot record, the distributor's catalogue and the final episode establish the span of the series.

- **27 Sep 2012. The pilot on CBS.** The Paley Center records the first broadcast of the pilot and identifies Jonny Lee Miller and Lucy Liu in the cast. [Paley Center pilot record](https://www.paleycenter.org/collection/item?item=108274&p=128&q=cbs)
- **2012 to 2019. The full run.** Paramount's distribution catalogue describes the series as a contemporary take on Sherlock Holmes and Joan Watson in New York. [Paramount distribution catalogue](https://paramountglobalcontent.com/title/elementary)
- **15 Aug 2019. Their Last Bow.** The episode catalogue dates Their Last Bow to August 2019 and lists it as episode 13 of season seven. [Apple TV episode listing](https://tv.apple.com/us/episode/their-last-bow/umc.cmc.2qc5amgikwqagsavfhcfbssnf)

The broadcast and episode records give the two endpoints. The distribution catalogue describes the series as a whole. [Full timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/companies/elementary-series-history/)

## Three Eagles album releases

Three catalogue entries trace the band's studio releases from its debut through Hotel California to Long Road Out of Eden.

- **1 Jun 1972. Eagles.** The band's self-titled debut includes Take It Easy and Peaceful Easy Feeling. [Apple Music catalogue entry](https://music.apple.com/us/album/eagles/635791801)
- **8 Dec 1976. Hotel California.** The album catalogue dates Hotel California to December 1976 and lists nine tracks, including its title song. [Apple Music catalogue entry](https://music.apple.com/us/album/hotel-california/635770200)
- **2007. Long Road Out of Eden.** The band's discography lists Long Road Out of Eden in 2007, after the 1994 release Hell Freezes Over. [Eagles official discography](https://eagles.com/pages/releases)

These are album release records. A release date and the date of a recording session answer different questions. [Full timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/companies/eagles-albums-recording-history/)

## SpaceX from Falcon 1 to crew flight

NASA-hosted records trace a launch to orbit, a cargo demonstration and a crewed test flight.

- **Sep 2008. Falcon 1 reaches orbit.** NASA's mission press kit records that Falcon 1 reached orbit. [NASA mission press kit](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/spacex_nasa_crs-4_presskit.pdf)
- **May 2012. Dragon reaches the station.** An uncrewed Dragon demonstration reached the International Space Station, delivered cargo and returned to Earth. [NASA mission press kit](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/spacex_nasa_crs-4_presskit.pdf)
- **30 May 2020. Crew Dragon launches.** NASA astronauts Robert Behnken and Douglas Hurley launched on the Demo-2 test mission to the space station. [NASA launch announcement](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-astronauts-launch-from-america-in-historic-test-flight-of-spacex-crew-dragon/)

The dates mark different milestones. The 2012 demonstration carried cargo. The 2020 test flight carried astronauts. [Full timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/companies/spacex/)

## Three decisions in the Apple Ireland case

The dispute concerned Irish tax rulings for two Apple entities. These records follow the EU state aid decision and its review by two courts.

- **30 Aug 2016. Commission decision.** The European Commission found that the tax rulings gave the Apple entities unlawful state aid and ordered Ireland to recover it. [Commission decision (EU) 2017/1283](https://ec.europa.eu/competition/state_aid/cases/253200/253200_1851004_674_2.pdf)
- **15 Jul 2020. General Court annulment.** The EU General Court annulled the Commission decision in joined cases T-778/16 and T-892/16. [General Court press release](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2020-07/cp200090en.pdf)
- **10 Sep 2024. Court of Justice judgment.** In C-465/20 P, the Court of Justice set aside the 2020 judgment and confirmed the Commission decision. [Court of Justice press release](https://curia.europa.eu/site/upload/docs/application/pdf/2024-09/cp240133en.pdf)

The 2020 annulment was reversed on appeal in 2024. The Court of Justice ruling concerns EU state aid. [Full timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/court-cases/apple-ireland-state-aid/)

## How to read a sourced timeline

Start with the date, open the record cited for that event and check whether a later entry changed the position.

- **Step 1. Read the date as shown.** A date may give a year, a month or a day. Treat each entry at the precision its record supports. [How It Unfolded methodology](https://howitunfolded.com/methodology/)
- **Step 2. Open the cited record.** Read the document for the event it supports. A regulator's immediate action and a later investigation finding answer different questions. [Flight 1282 timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/incidents/alaska-airlines-1282-door-plug/)
- **Step 3. Check for a later decision.** A later court can change an earlier result. Compare the decision date and the court before describing the outcome. [Apple Ireland timeline and sources](https://howitunfolded.com/court-cases/apple-ireland-state-aid/)

A timeline gives the order of events. A causal explanation needs evidence that connects them. [Publisher methodology](https://howitunfolded.com/methodology/)
